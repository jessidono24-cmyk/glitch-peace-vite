## Key Findings

### The Circadian Clock
1. The **suprachiasmatic nucleus (SCN)** in the hypothalamus is the master pacemaker
2. Period length: approximately **24.2 hours** (slightly longer than Earth day)
3. Light is the primary **zeitgeber** (time-giver) that resets the clock daily
4. Internal clock persists in complete darkness — it is endogenous, not reactive

### Cognitive Performance Rhythms
Research-established circadian effects on cognition:
- **Peak alertness**: ~2–4 hours after habitual wake time
- **Post-lunch dip**: ~2–3pm — increased sleepiness, reduced vigilance (independent of meal)
- **Evening analytical peak**: Many adults show best abstract reasoning ~6–8pm
- **Memory consolidation**: Primarily occurs during slow-wave sleep (first half of night)
- **Creative insight**: Higher in hypnagogic states (falling asleep) and REM sleep

### Chronotypes
- **Morning types** ("larks"): Peak performance earlier; ~25% of population
- **Evening types** ("owls"): Peak performance later; ~25% of population
- **Intermediate types**: The remaining ~50%
- Chronotype is partly genetic (CLOCK, PER3 genes) and shifts across lifespan (teens: owls; elderly: larks)

### Ultradian Rhythms
Within each day, 90-minute ultradian cycles (Kleitman):
- ~90 minutes of higher alertness alternating with ~20-minute lower alertness
- Nasal breathing switches sides every ~90 minutes (mirrors brain hemisphere alternation)
- Optimal work intervals align with ultradian peaks: 90-minute focused work, 20-minute rest
- Disrupting ultradian rhythm (working through rest phases) degrades performance

### Circadian Disruption & Health
- Chronic disruption (shift work, jet lag, social jetlag) is associated with:
  - Increased addiction vulnerability (Hasler et al., 2012)
  - Elevated cortisol and reduced immune function
  - Impaired memory consolidation
  - Depressive symptoms
- Recovery of normal rhythm takes **days to weeks** after disruption

### Planetary Hours (Traditional System)
The classical 7-planet hour system maps days and hours to archetypal qualities:
| Day | Ruler | Qualities |
|-----|-------|-----------|
| Sunday | Sun | Vitality, consciousness, illumination |
| Monday | Moon | Intuition, emotion, receptivity |
| Tuesday | Mars | Action, challenge, confrontation |
| Wednesday | Mercury | Communication, learning, connection |
| Thursday | Jupiter | Expansion, wisdom, abundance |
| Friday | Venus | Beauty, harmony, relationship |
| Saturday | Saturn | Structure, discipline, limitation |

Traditional planetary hour sequence within each day follows the Chaldean order: Saturn → Jupiter → Mars → Sun → Venus → Mercury → Moon → (repeat)

**Evidence basis**: The modern 7-day week maps directly to the classical planetary rulers (Sun-day, Mon-day, Satur-day, etc.) — this system has documented cultural continuity since ~300 BCE Babylon.

## Mechanic Implications for Temporal System

### Daily Challenge Unlocks
- Each day's challenges should resonate with planetary ruler quality:
  - **Sun-day**: Illumination challenges (constellation mode, light-based puzzles)
  - **Moon-day**: Intuition challenges (pattern recognition without explicit hints)
  - **Mars-day**: Confrontation challenges (shooter mode, enemy-heavy grid)
  - **Mercury-day**: Learning challenges (vocabulary/logic puzzles, ornithology IDs)
  - **Jupiter-day**: Expansion challenges (large maps, abundance mechanics)
  - **Venus-day**: Harmony challenges (rhythm mode, meditation mode)
  - **Saturn-day**: Discipline challenges (minimal-tile survival mode, constraint puzzles)

### Ultradian Session Design
- Optimal session length: **20–25 minutes** (single ultradian work unit, aligns with Pomodoro evidence)
- Sessions should escalate difficulty for first 15 minutes, then ease for final 5 (matches natural ultradian arc)
- After session: 5-minute "integration pause" before next session is recommended by science

### Circadian Context
- Game could ask for time-of-day and adjust challenge type:
  - **Morning**: Higher precision tasks (grid mode, ornithology identification)
  - **Afternoon dip**: Lower cognitive load (rhythm mode, meditation mode)
  - **Evening peak**: Abstract/creative challenges (alchemy transmutation, constellation mapping)
- Optional: gentle notification if playing during typical sleep window (care for user wellbeing)

### Temporal Rhythm Mechanic
- Planetary quests unlock on their resonant day and provide day-specific bonuses
- "Circadian bonus": Playing during player's habitual peak window gives temporary score multiplier
- "Rest integration": Returning after ≥8 hours since last session awards "dream consolidation bonus"
