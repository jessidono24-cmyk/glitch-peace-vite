## Key Findings
1. **Hebb's Rule**: "Neurons that fire together wire together" — repeated co-activation strengthens synaptic connections
2. Adult neurogenesis occurs in the hippocampus (dentate gyrus) — new neurons support new learning and memory
3. **Structural plasticity**: Jugglers show measurable gray matter increase in motion-processing areas after 3 months of training
4. **Critical periods**: Some plasticity windows close with development; but adult plasticity persists throughout life
5. Cognitive exercise, physical exercise, sleep, and novel experience are the four primary drivers of neuroplastic change

## Application in GLITCH·PEACE
- Core gameplay is a neuroplasticity engine: pattern recognition training drives measurable neural changes
- Progressive difficulty ensures continued challenge in the zone of proximal development
- Multi-modal stimulation (visual, auditory, motor) maximizes neural engagement and cross-modal plasticity
- Session-based design with rest intervals supports memory consolidation during sleep
