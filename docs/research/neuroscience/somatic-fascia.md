## Key Findings

### Fascia as a Sensory Organ
1. Fascia (connective tissue) contains **10× more sensory receptors** than muscle
2. Acts as a whole-body proprioceptive net — the "felt sense" of body position
3. Fascial tension directly modulates emotional state via interoceptive signals
4. Trauma is stored as **chronic fascial holding patterns** (restricted movement, bracing)
5. Slow, mindful movement releases fascial holding and down-regulates the nervous system

### Somatic Memory
- Emotional experiences are encoded in body posture and movement patterns
- The body "remembers" past threat responses (freeze, collapse, bracing)
- Release through movement — not just cognitive understanding — is required for healing
- Breath is the bridge between voluntary (somatic) and autonomic nervous systems

### Polyvagal Theory (Porges)
- The vagus nerve has **two branches** mediating different social/threat responses:
  - **Ventral vagal** (safe, social, playful): Regulated state, connection
  - **Dorsal vagal** (shutdown, freeze): Immobilization, dissociation
  - **Sympathetic** (fight/flight): Mobilization, action
- Safety cues ("neuroception") shift the nervous system without conscious thought
- **Prosody, rhythm, and gentle movement** are direct vagal toning mechanisms

### Interoception
- Interoception = sensing internal body states (heart rate, breath, gut, muscle tension)
- Poor interoception is associated with emotional dysregulation and addiction
- Training interoception improves emotional resilience and self-regulation
- Mindful body scans measurably increase interoceptive accuracy

## Mechanic Implications for Meditation Mode

### Breath Prompt System
- Breath prompts should be **rhythmic and predictable** — activates ventral vagal (safe state)
- 4-7-8 or box-breathing patterns are evidence-based for parasympathetic activation
- Visual breath guides (expanding/contracting shapes) mirror natural diaphragmatic rhythm
- Disrupted breathing = stress cue → tile damage or reduced regeneration rate

### Body Scan Tiles
- Body scan mechanic: Player "sweeps" attention through body regions top-to-bottom
- Each successfully attended region increases **somatic awareness score**
- Tension tiles (red/orange glitch tiles) can be "released" through sustained attention
- Models the actual clinical intervention of body scanning for trauma release

### Grounding Mechanics
- Grounding = orienting to present moment through physical sensation
- 5-4-3-2-1 sensory grounding (5 sights, 4 sounds, 3 touches, 2 smells, 1 taste)
- In-game: Environment shifts to highlight 5 distinct sensory cues during grounding sequence
- Successfully grounding prevents health drain during DESPAIR tile exposure

### Embodiment Score
- Persistent score tracking how "embodied" the player is during sessions
- High embodiment: Breath synchronized, body scan complete, grounding active
- Low embodiment: Erratic movement, skipped prompts, rapid unchecked tile exposure
- Embodiment score directly scales health regeneration rate

## Falsifiability
If breath prompts are purely decorative (no mechanical effect), the somatic model is not implemented. Breath state should measurably affect game state.
