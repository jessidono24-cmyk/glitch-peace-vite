## Core Concepts

### Four Noble Truths
1. **Dukkha** (suffering/unsatisfactoriness): The nature of conditioned existence
2. **Samudaya** (origin): Craving (tanha) and clinging are the root of suffering
3. **Nirodha** (cessation): Suffering can end — there is a way out
4. **Magga** (path): The Eightfold Path leads to liberation

### The Three Marks of Existence
- **Anicca** (impermanence): All phenomena arise and pass; nothing is permanent
- **Dukkha** (unsatisfactoriness): Clinging to impermanent things causes suffering
- **Anatta** (non-self): No fixed, permanent self — identity is a dynamic process

### The Eightfold Path
Right View, Right Intention, Right Speech, Right Action, Right Livelihood, Right Effort, Right Mindfulness, Right Concentration

### Dependent Origination (Paticca-samuppada)
All phenomena arise in dependence upon conditions; nothing exists independently. The chain of 12 links describes how suffering perpetuates itself and how it can be unwound.
