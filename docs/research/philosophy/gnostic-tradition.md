## Core Concepts

### The Pleroma and Kenoma
- **Pleroma** (fullness): The divine realm of light, completeness, pure consciousness
- **Kenoma** (deficiency): The material world — a diminished, fallen reflection of the Pleroma
- Human souls are sparks of divine light trapped in material existence

### The Demiurge
- The creator of the material world is not the ultimate divine — it is a lesser, flawed deity
- Material suffering is not a divine punishment but a structural feature of limited consciousness
- Liberation requires recognizing the Demiurge and transcending its domain

### Gnosis (Knowledge)
- **Gnosis** = direct experiential knowledge of one's divine nature (not faith or belief)
- Salvation through self-knowledge: "Know yourself and you will know the universe"
- The goal is **awakening** (anagnorisis — recognition) to one's true nature

### The Archons
- Archons = lesser powers that maintain the material prison; each corresponds to a planetary sphere
- Liberation involves passing through each archonic layer (analogous to chakras in reverse)

## Application in GLITCH·PEACE
- **Glitch metaphor**: The game world is a "glitch" — a fracture revealing the Pleroma beneath
- **Archetype powers**: Each archetype represents a Gnostic self-knowledge path
- **Matrix A/B**: Matrix A = Kenoma (material, habitual); Matrix B = glimpse of Pleroma (awakened state)
- **Peace nodes as Pleroma sparks**: Collecting them = gathering your scattered divine light
- **Despair/Rage tiles as Archons**: Material forces that must be transcended, not destroyed
- **Campaign arc**: Movement from Kenoma → Pleroma (emergence Level 1 → 5)
