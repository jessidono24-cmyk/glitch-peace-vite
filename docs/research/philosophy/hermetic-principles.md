## The Seven Hermetic Principles

1. **Mentalism** ("The All is Mind"): Reality emerges from consciousness; mind precedes matter
2. **Correspondence** ("As above, so below"): Patterns repeat across scales (fractal self-similarity)
3. **Vibration** ("Nothing rests; everything moves"): All is frequency and movement
4. **Polarity** ("Everything is dual"): Opposites are identical in nature, differing in degree
5. **Rhythm** ("Everything flows"): The pendulum swing; action and reaction
6. **Cause & Effect** ("Every cause has its effect"): Nothing happens by chance
7. **Gender** ("Gender is in everything"): Creative/receptive poles in all phenomena
