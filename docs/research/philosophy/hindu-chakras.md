## Key Principles
1. Chakras are **energy centers** in the subtle body — not physically localized but mapped to body regions
2. **Kundalini** energy rises from root to crown through spiritual development
3. Imbalance (too much or too little energy) in any chakra creates characteristic dysfunction
4. Practices (yoga, breathwork, meditation) restore chakra balance
