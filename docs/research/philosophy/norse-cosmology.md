# Norse Cosmology

## Sources
- Sturluson, S. (c.1220). *Prose Edda* (trans. Byock, J., 2005). Penguin Classics.

## Key Concepts

### Yggdrasil (World Tree)
- Cosmic axis connecting Nine Worlds (Asgard, Midgard, Helheim, etc.)
- Three roots: one in Asgard (heaven), one in Jotunheim (giants), one in Niflheim (underworld)
- Three wells: Urðarbrunnr (fate), Mímisbrunnr (wisdom), Hvergelmir (primordial waters)
- Ratatoskr (squirrel) runs messages between eagle (sky) and Níðhöggr (serpent at root)

### The Nine Worlds
| World | Inhabitants | Realm |
|-------|-------------|-------|
| Asgard | Gods (Aesir) | Upper |
| Midgard | Humans | Middle |
| Jotunheim | Giants | Middle |
| Alfheim | Light elves | Upper |
| Svartalfheim | Dark elves/dwarves | Lower |
| Helheim | Dishonored dead | Lower |
| Niflheim | Primordial ice | Lower |
| Muspelheim | Primordial fire | Upper |
| Vanaheim | Nature gods | Middle |

### The Norns (Fate Weavers)
- Urð (past/that which was), Verðandi (present/that which is), Skuld (future/that which shall be)
- Weave the wyrd (fate/web of causation) — no action is without consequence

### Ragnarök
- Cyclical destruction and renewal — not final ending but transformation
- Giants (chaos) overcome gods (order) temporarily; new world emerges purified
