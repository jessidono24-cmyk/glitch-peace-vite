## Core Principles

### The Dichotomy of Control
- **Up to us**: Judgments, desires, aversions, impulses — our internal response
- **Not up to us**: Body, reputation, property, outcome — external circumstances
- Suffering arises from treating "not up to us" as if it were "up to us"

### The Four Virtues
1. **Wisdom** (sophia): Knowing what is truly good
2. **Justice** (dikaiosyne): Treating others rightly
3. **Courage** (andreia): Acting rightly despite fear
4. **Temperance** (sophrosyne): Moderation; not being ruled by desires

### Memento Mori & Amor Fati
- **Memento Mori**: Remember death — perspective-giving practice; reduces trivial anxieties
- **Amor Fati** (Nietzsche's extension): Love of fate — embrace what happens as what should happen

### The View from Above
Meditative practice: imagine the whole of human affairs from cosmic perspective; your concerns shrink to their actual size.

## Application in GLITCH·PEACE
- **Dichotomy of Control**: Player cannot control tile distribution (not up to us); only response matters (up to us) — impulse buffer mechanic
- **Four Virtues**: Archetype selection maps to virtue emphasis (Healer = Justice, Warrior = Courage, Sage = Wisdom, etc.)
- **Memento Mori**: Health bar as mortality reminder; consequence preview as memento mori practice
- **Amor Fati**: Embracing a bad tile draw without complaint — equanimity training
- **View from Above**: End-of-session stats screen as stoic reflection practice
