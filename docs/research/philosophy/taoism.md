## Core Concepts

### The Tao
- Tao (道) = "The Way" — the underlying principle of reality, preceding and encompassing all
- Cannot be named or fully described; any description is partial
- "The Tao that can be told is not the eternal Tao" (Tao Te Ching, 1)

### Wu Wei (Non-Action)
- **Wu wei** (无为) = effortless action aligned with natural flow
- Not passivity, but acting without resistance — like water finding its path
- Maximum effect from minimum force when aligned with the Tao

### The Three Treasures
1. **Compassion** (ci 慈): Loving-kindness toward all beings
2. **Frugality** (jian 俭): Simplicity, not excess
3. **Humility** (bu gan wei tianxia xian 不敢為天下先): Not presuming to be first

### Yin-Yang Dynamics
- All opposites are complementary, not contradictory
- Each contains the seed of its opposite; nothing is purely one thing
- Dynamic balance — not static equilibrium
